# Colonist Summary

Adds a Summary tab, in the look of the UI Rethemed mods, that shows every colonist on one row, with their title after the name as on the vanilla Work tab. The Summary page shows ideoligion and xenotype (with Ideology or Biotech), age, the fight-or-flee setting (changeable right there), what each colonist is doing, mood against the break thresholds, food, rest and recreation, every skill with its passion, weapon, worn apparel (lined up by slot, head to toe), overall sharp, blunt and heat armor and comfortable temperature range. The Health page shows overall health, pain, every capacity, bleeding with time until death, infections, what needs tending, the medicine and self-tend settings (changeable right there), missing body parts, implants, chronic and other conditions, and queued operations with why the next can't start and a button to add one; prisoners and, optionally, animals are listed too. The title bar counts what matters on each page, with a search and a sort menu. Hover over anything for details, click an item to open its info card, and click a column heading to sort by it (a third click returns to the usual order). The mod settings set the window size and which columns show.
