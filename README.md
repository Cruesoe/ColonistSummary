# Colony Summary

Adds a Summary tab, in the look of the UI Rethemed mods, that shows every colonist on one row: ideoligion and xenotype (with Ideology or Biotech), age, the fight-or-flee setting (changeable right there), bleeding with time until death, every skill with its passion, weapon, worn apparel, overall sharp, blunt and heat armor and comfortable temperature range (green while comfortable). The title bar counts colonists, the bleeding and the uncomfortable, with a search and a sort menu. Hover over anything for details, click an item to open its info card, and click a column heading to sort by it. Colonists in caravans and transporters are listed too.
